How We Do It

The Art of the Craft

01
🌾
Italian Flour
We import Caputo "00" flour from Naples — the only grain that delivers the perfect leopard char and airy crumb.
02
🫙
72h Ferment
Our heritage sourdough starter, named Rosa, rests for three days developing complexity and a featherlight structure.
03
🤲
Hand Stretched
Never rolled, never pinned. Each disc is coaxed open by hand to preserve every last gas bubble.
04
🔥
Stone Oven
90 seconds at 900°F in our Neapolitan oven. Blistered, charred, and gloriously alive.
